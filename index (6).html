Medz Ron Macasaet

Personal Finance Educator · Author · Financial Services Manager

Born in Olongapo City. Raised by an OFW father and a manicurist mother. Pandesal sa utang ang almusal.

Today — author, financial coach, property investor, and founder of SmartPera. This is not a story of overnight success. Ito ay isang patunay na kahit mahirap ang simula, may pagbabago kung willing kang kumilos.

🏙
1986 — OLONGAPO CITY
Born pangalawa sa bunso sa anim na magkakapatid. Father OFW sa Kuwait. Walang ipon. Walang insurance. Pandesal sa utang ang almusal. But his parents valued education above all.
✈️
2010 — SINGAPORE
Left the Philippines as an OFW with no savings. Just dreams. Started in retail. Became one of the top salespersons. Knocked on doors. Surveyed streets.
💡
2014 — LIFE-CHANGING REALISATION
Medz realised he had been sending money home for years — but had no real asset generating income in his own name. This was the turning point that defined his entire career.
🎓
2021 — RFP GRADUATE
Graduated as an RFP Philippines Graduate — the premier financial planning credential in the country. A badge carried with humility and purpose.
🚀
2024–2026 — SMARTPERA
Promoted to Financial Services Manager. Founded SmartPera. Authored "Bakit Broke Pa Rin Ang Pinoy?" — a gift to every Filipino struggling financially. A roadmap. A spark. A legacy.
