10 Hard Truths Na Kailangang Marinig Bago Ka Magsimula

"Hindi ka mahirap dahil sa sweldo mo. Mahirap ka dahil walang nagturo sa'yo kung paano hawakan ang pera mo."

Reason #1
Walang Nagturo Sa'tin Kung Paano Hawakan Ang Pera
25% lang ng adult Filipinos ang financially literate. Sa 2021, bumaba pa ito sa 2%. Hindi ka nag-iisa.
Source: S&P Global Financial Literacy Survey; BSP Financial Inclusion Survey 2021
Reason #2
Ang Remittance Mo, ATM Ng Buong Pamilya!
78.6% ng cash remittances napupunta sa consumption. 75% ng OFW households? Zero ang na-invest.
Source: BSP Discussion Paper No. 2024-18; PSA Survey on Overseas Filipinos 2008-2022
Reason #3
Pakikisama Culture: Hindi Makatanggi Ang Pilipino
Pag tumanggi ka, "Mayabang na." The cycle of giving without limits destroys your future.
Reason #4
Bumibili Ng Pamporma, Hindi Ng Pampayamang Assets
18-22% ng remittance napupunta sa gadgets at appliances. 6% lang sa productive investments.
Reason #5
Biktima Ng Scam Dahil Gutom Sa Easy Money
₱60 billion ang nawala ng mga Pilipino sa Ponzi at pyramid schemes sa nakaraang dekada. OFWs ang primary target.
Source: SEC Philippines Investment Scam Advisories 2023; NBI Reports
Reason #6
Walang Emergency Fund, Insurance, Safety Net
45% ng Pilipino umuutang bilang first response sa emergency. 37% lang may savings sa bangko.
Source: BSP Financial Inclusion Survey 2021; BSP Consumer Expectations Survey
Reason #7
Walang Retirement Plan — Si Anak Ang "Plano"
Average SSS pension: ₱5,000/month. Poverty threshold ng isang pamilya: ₱13,000-14,000. Hindi sapat.
Reason #8
Overconfidence Sa Pag-Uwi: "₱5M, Kaya Na!"
83% ng returning OFWs walang trabaho within 3 months. Ang ₱5M ay hindi ₱5M sa loob ng 25 taon.
Reason #9
Sobrang Asa Sa State Pension
Ang state benefits ay supplement — hindi ang main plan. Ikaw ang main plan. Ang investments mo.
Reason #10
Hindi Naiintindihan Ang Inflation — The Silent Killer
₱50,000 ngayon = ₱18,756 na lang ang real value sa loob ng 25 taon. Kailangan mong i-invest, hindi lang mag-ipon.

Medz Ron Macasaet

Born in Olongapo City, Philippines. Raised by an OFW father and a manicurist mother. Pandesal sa utang ang almusal. Today — author, financial coach, property investor, and founder of SmartPera.

This is not a story of overnight success. Ito ay isang patunay na kahit mahirap ang simula, may pagbabago kung willing kang kumilos.

The Journey

From sleeping on the floor to building multiple income-generating assets

1986 — Olongapo City
Born pangalawa sa bunso sa anim na magkakapatid. Father OFW sa Kuwait. Mother, isang manicurist. Walang ipon. Walang insurance. Pandesal sa utang ang almusal. Nakikinood ng TV sa kapitbahay. Tansan bilang laruan. But his parents valued education above all.
Teen Years — The Seed
Naging kasambahay para sa kapatid para makapag-aral. His older brother — a bank employee — taught him early: "Hindi sapat ang sipag. Dapat may diskarte ka rin." Those words changed everything.
College & Early Career
Juggled studies and night shifts at a fast food chain. After graduating — KFC manager, Penshoppe, ABS-CBN sales, MLM, sister's bakery. Ang tanging puhunan: pag-asa. Failure after failure. But never quit.
2010 — Singapore
Left the Philippines as an OFW with no savings. Just dreams. Started in retail. Became one of the top salespersons. Knocked on doors. Surveyed streets.
2013 — Financial Services
Joined the financial services industry. Began studying financial planning in Singapore deeply.
2014 — Life-Changing Realisation
The year everything changed. Medz realised he had been sending money home for years — but had no real asset generating income in his own name. Nagsimula siyang mag-knock on doors, mag-prospect, at pag-aralan nang mabuti ang financial planning. This was the turning point that defined his entire career.
2017 — Personal Milestone
Married Marionne, his partner in life and values. From sleeping on the floor to multiple income-generating assets — property investments in Pampanga, Cebu, and Bataan.
2021 — RFP Graduate
Graduated as a Registered Financial Planner (RFP) Philippines — the premier financial planning credential in the country. A badge carried with humility and purpose.
2024–2026 — SmartPera
Promoted to Financial Services Manager. Founded SmartPera. Authored "Bakit Broke Pa Rin Ang Pinoy?" — a gift to every Filipino struggling financially. A roadmap. A spark. A legacy.
